How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

No complex math needed. Here is why combining 3 sensors solves the problem completely.

🏎️

1. The Wheels (Speed)

Like human legs. They measure how fast the robot moves by counting tire turns.

The Problem: On smooth or dusty bunker floors, wheels slip. The robot thinks it is moving forward, but it's sliding!
🧭

2. The Gyroscope (Balance)

Like our inner ears. It senses turns, spins, and tilts 100 times per second.

The Superpower: It never slips! Even if the wheels spin out on ice, the gyro knows the exact direction the robot is facing.
👁️

3. The Camera (Vision)

Like our eyes. It recognizes wall corners, doorways, and pillars to pinpoint exact location.

The Problem: In unlit hallways or smoke, cameras go blind. When that happens, the robot leans on its gyro.
🧠

The Secret: "Sensor Fusion" (Extended Kalman Filter)

Instead of trusting one sensor, the robot runs a smart filter 20 times every second. If the wheels start slipping on mud, the filter automatically ignores the wheels and trusts the gyro. If the lights go out, it relies on balance until the camera spots a light again. Result: The robot never crashes.
