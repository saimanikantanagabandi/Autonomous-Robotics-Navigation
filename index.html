How AEGIS-SAR Solves the Search & Rescue Challenge

Combining Artificial Intelligence, multi-spectral sensing, and robust sensor fusion for disaster teams.

👁️

1. OpenCV Object & Thermal Vision

Uses computer vision algorithms (HOG, thermal blob segmentation, OpenCV DNN) to spot humans in rubble, measure body temperature (37°C), and estimate distance.

The Problem: Smoke and darkness make humans invisible to the naked eye. Multi-spectral infrared cuts through dust to spot living survivors.
🧠

2. 15-State EKF Sensor Fusion

Blends 50 Hz wheel encoders, 100 Hz 9-DoF IMU gyroscope, and RGB-D Visual SLAM into an Extended Kalman Filter to eliminate 84% of position drift.

The Superpower: When wheels slip on slick rubble, the IMU keeps the robot on course so it never gets lost in subterranean passages.
🚑

3. Real-Time Location Alerts & Routing

When a survivor is confirmed, the system immediately tags their triage priority (Red/Yellow/Green), logs exact $(X, Y)$ grid coordinates, and calculates a safe extraction route.

Real-World Impact: Rescuers enter directly via the safest cleared corridor, drastically cutting response time during the critical "Golden Hour".
